Your questions, answered

What a kanban board is (Q6)

A kanban board is a board with one column per stage: Open, In Progress, Parked, Done. Every item is a card. You drag a card to the next column as the work moves along, and up or down to set its order. That is all "kanban" means.

What you asked for is the same board split into rows, one row per brand or per person, so you can see each person's cards across all stages at once. That is being added as a Group by switch at the top of the board: None, Brand, or Person. The filters you already have (brand, person, category, personal, text) still work on top of it, and the board remembers your last choice.

What IT needs to grant, and why (Q11)

Claude reads your mailbox through a Microsoft 365 connection that IT approved as read-only. That is enough for the two-hour digest. It is not enough to flag an email or to put a draft in your Drafts folder.

To unlock flagging and drafting, an admin (Doug or Rob) adds one permission to that connection: Mail.ReadWrite. Where: Microsoft Entra admin center → Enterprise applications → the Claude app → Permissions → Grant admin consent. It takes a few minutes. We are not asking for Mail.Send, because Virginia never sends email for you.

Two small, optional extras IT could grant at the same time: People.Read (lets Claude look up a coworker's address) and Team.ReadBasic.All (lets Claude list your Teams). Neither is required.

The 7 am brief by email (Q14) is separate. The cleanest route needs no IT at all: a small email service account in your name that the app uses to email you. That is decision R3 below.
